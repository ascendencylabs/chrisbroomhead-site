## Who processes data for me

- **Calendly** — scheduling and booking forms
- **Stripe** — payments
- **Zoom** — video consultations
- **Google Workspace** — email
- **Supabase** — storage of form sign-ups (servers in Singapore)
- **GitHub Pages** — website hosting

Some of these providers store data outside the UK. Where they do, transfers rely on the safeguards the provider offers, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or standard contractual clauses.

I do not sell your data.

## How long I keep it

- Form sign-ups: until you ask me to delete them, or 3 years after your last contact, whichever is sooner.
- Consultation records and health information: 6 years after your last consultation, then deleted.
- Payment records: as required by UK tax law (currently 6 years).

If you are unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) or 0303 123 1113.
